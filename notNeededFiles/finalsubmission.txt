Final submission. (deadline August 1 evening)
Submit your complete code as a .zip archive together with your trained model weights, runnable through the provided inference interface. 
We must be able to reproduce your reported results from this code and a README. 
The README must explain how to set up the environment and how to obtain and preprocess the data. 
If you collect images from the internet, a list of source URLs is sufficient. 
Develop your project in a GitHub repository and grant us access to it after the submission. 
Include the GitHub repository link in your final report.
 In addition, submit a final report of 8 pages, double column,
excluding references, written in the provided template and following a scientific paper structure with the sections Introduction, Related Work, Method, Experiments, and Conclusions. 
The Experiments section shall include the results of your empirical analyses, evaluations, 
and the explainable AI (XAI) analysis. 
Finally, please add a detailed contributions list in the appendix stating what each member did. 

Your grade does not depend on the raw recognition performance you reach on the held-out test set. 
It reflects the quality of your work and your development process: 
the ideas you explore, your motivation, the soundness of your execution 
and the depth of your empirical analysis. 
Approaches that you tried and that did not improve your results are equally valuable, 
as long as you analyse and discuss them.
